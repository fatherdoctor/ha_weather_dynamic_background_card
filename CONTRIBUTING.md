# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help!

- **Bugs:** open an issue with your Home Assistant version, browser/app, the card's YAML and any errors from the browser console (F12). The console should show `HA-WEATHER-DYNAMIC-BACKGROUND-CARD <version>`.
- **Pull requests:** the card is a single, dependency-free file, `ha-weather-dynamic-background-card.js`. Please keep it that way. Check your change in Home Assistant in light and dark mode, and with *Reduce motion* on.
